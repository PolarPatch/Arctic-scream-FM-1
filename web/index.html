ARCTICSCREAM

Dark ambient drones and feedback screams from the arctic tundra. Slow, cold drones that hum for minutes, and a voice that wakes on its own and screams. Play it here: the panel below runs the same code as the instrument. Pick a demo, or switch it on and play the keys.

01

CONTROLS

Click the panel, or use the computer's keyboard: the keys below are written on the panel too. Drag or scroll a knob to turn it. Right-click or Shift-click a button to hold it.

KEYS

Z–/ S–'
The keys F2 to A♯3. An octave down, for drones.
Q–O
B3 to G4, in semitones.
LATCH ON
Press a key once to start a note, again to stop it. The six Holvut sounds latch.
hold SEL
Chord mode: one key, a whole chord.
hold ARP
The arpeggiator plays the keys held (with LATCH: they stay).
hold GLO + a key
White keys 1–4 mute T1–T4, 5–8 solo them.

BUTTONS

1 FX
TAPE, GRAIN, DELAY, SPACE. Held: PERFORM, eight tricks on the keys.
2 SCL
SCALE (the keys snap to it, HORROR last), then CHORD.
3 ENV
ATTACK, DECAY, SUSTAIN, RELEASE.
4 LFO
RATE, DEPTH, DESTINATION, SHAPE. Again: DRONE.
5 EDIT
The source's four knobs, then SOURCE and LATCH. Held + OCT: undo, redo.
6 GLO
SONG: sections, patterns, repeats.
7 HOME
Every track's wave, then MIXER 1 and 2. Held: SYSTEM.
8 SAVE
SOUNDS (64 slots) and PROJECTS. Held + a key: recall or save slots A–D.
9 ARP · 0 SEQ · - PLAY · = REC
The arpeggiator, the sequencer's grid, start and stop, record.

KNOBS

KNOB 1–4
The four values at the bottom of the screen, on every page.
← → ALGORITHM
Picks the track, T1–T4. Every page shows that track.
↑ ↓ SELECT

PRESETS

02

SOURCES

Eight sound sources, one per track, each with four knobs. Six voices are shared by the four tracks. EDIT twice for SOURCE, KNOB 1 to pick.

Holvut

FEEDBACKNEWNORTH SAMI: TO HOWL

A string and a resonant body, trapped in a feedback loop with saturation, filters and diffusion. Nothing is struck. Below the threshold the loop sleeps; past it, it wakes and howls by itself. Each key sends a burst into the loop, so a note near the edge provokes it. After the design of the Synthux Audrey II, written anew.

FEEDBACK · BODY · DAMP · DRIVE

ANALOGFILTER

03

DRONE

Press LFO twice: a sound with a life of its own. The knobs have North Sami names. At 0 % the sound is exactly as set; the settings are saved with the sound.

KNOB 1SNOWDRIFT
BORGA

Four slow random walks move the filter, the source's shape and its most natural knob, and the reverb's distance. Each voice drifts a few cents and breathes, so a held chord sounds like a choir. It never repeats.

KNOB 2STORM
STOARBMA

The tension. It opens the filter, raises resonance and drive, spreads the voices and makes the walks deeper and faster.

KNOB 3TO WAKE
GOHCCÁT

1 to 32 bars for the storm to rise, in tempo. A drone woken from silence starts calm and builds.

KNOB 4HOLD
LATCH

Press once to start a note, again to stop it. For drones that hum for minutes.

04

SEQUENCER

Sixty-four steps on every track, chords and an arpeggiator, and a song mode that chains the slots.

GRID

SEQ64 STEPS

NOTE, CHANCE, RATCHET and LENGTH per step. Hold SEL and turn a knob to lock the sound to a step.

RECORD

RECLIVE · STEP

Stopped + REC: each key fills the next step. Playing + REC: notes land on the nearest step.

PATTERN

SEQ TWICE

STEPS, DIVISION (1/4 to 1/32T), SWING and PLAY MODE: forward, backward, ping-pong, random.

SONG

GLOSLOTS A–D

Chain your patterns into a song: sections, patterns and repeats. REC + PLAY records the slots you switch to.

STATUS

Version 1.1. Free and open source, GPL-3.0. Unofficial firmware, not made or endorsed by M-VAVE or GR!MM: install at your own risk.

INSTALL

Download arctic-scream-1.1.fwsc from the releases, connect the FM-1 over USB, then:

pip install mido python-rtmidi
python3 tools/fm1_install.py arctic-scream-1.1.fwsc

Stuck? The Rescue page on the FM-1 Firmware Hub brings it back.
